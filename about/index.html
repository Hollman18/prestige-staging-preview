About

One of us sells homes. One of us styles them.

Prestige is the two of us, and it started the way most family businesses do: over a kitchen table. One of us spent years as a realtor and running new construction and flip projects, and learned from the inside what actually makes a house sell. The other could never walk into a room without moving something, and built a career out of how a space makes a person feel.

We put the two together. Strategy on one side, emotion on the other, and a standard neither of us is willing to drop. We own our inventory and we employ our own crew, so every piece that goes into your home is one we chose, moved and placed ourselves.

We have staged for investors, for agents, and for families leaving a home they raised their children in. That last one we treat carefully. It is still your house until the day it is not.
